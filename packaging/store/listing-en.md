## Description [up to 10,000 characters]

Elanero is a full PDF editor for Windows. Open a PDF to read, mark up, edit, rearrange,
protect or convert it, all on your own computer.

Edit the document itself
• Change text and images in place, keeping the original fonts where possible.
• Add headers and footers, page numbers, Bates numbering and watermarks.

Comment and review
• Highlight, underline and strike out text, and add notes, text boxes, callouts, shapes,
  freehand ink and stamps, including your own image stamps.
• See every comment in one panel, reply to comments and filter them.

Organize pages
• Reorder, rotate, delete, extract and insert pages with drag and drop.
• Combine several files into one, or split a document into parts.

Convert and optimize
• Export to Word, Excel, HTML, Markdown, plain text and images.
• Reduce file size, and convert to PDF/A-2b for long-term archiving.

Protect and clean up
• Redact text and areas permanently, then check that nothing is left underneath.
• Remove hidden data such as metadata, attachments and scripts.
• Add password protection with AES-256 encryption.

Recognize text (OCR)
• Turn scanned pages into searchable, selectable text, with automatic deskewing. English is
  built in; other languages are a free download.

Also included
• Compare two versions of a document and see what changed.
• An accessibility checker, tag editor and experimental auto-tagging.
• Fast search, vector-quality printing, light and dark themes, keyboard shortcuts and a
  command palette (Ctrl+Shift+P).
• Several windows side by side, and recovery of unsaved work after a crash.

Private by design
Elanero works offline. It has no account, no telemetry and no ads, and your documents never
leave your computer. The only time it goes online is when you download an OCR language.

Open source
Elanero is free software under the GNU AGPL-3.0-or-later. Its source code is at
https://github.com/linguagraph/pdf-editor.

## FAQ / additional information

Uninstalling the app deletes its settings, recovery copies of unsaved documents and
downloaded OCR languages. Save your work before uninstalling.

## Notes for certification [Submission options ▸ Notes for certification]

Elanero is a full-trust desktop PDF editor. It needs `runFullTrust` because it opens and saves
files anywhere the user chooses, prints, starts additional windows of itself, and optionally
starts LibreOffice, if installed, to convert Office files. It doesn't need an account or test
credentials. To try it, open any PDF from File ▸ Open, or from Explorer with "Open with".
